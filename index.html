    Head angle

    How to measure

    1. Ear: tap the small flap just in front of your ear opening.
    2. Base of neck (C7): tap the bony bump where your neck meets your back, roughly level with the top of your shoulders. To find it, feel the back of your neck while you nod: C7 is the bump that stays put, and the one above it moves.

    The app measures the angle between that line and the horizontal. If you tap too low on the back, the angle reads too high.

    Taking the photo

    • Exactly side-on, about 2 m away, phone level at shoulder height.
    • Stand naturally and look straight ahead. Take a breath and let your shoulders relax first; don’t stand up extra tall.
    • Hair off your ear and neck, and a fitted top so C7 shows.
    • Also try one sitting at your desk after 20 minutes of work. It’s often lower than a standing photo.

    What to aim for

    <45°Forward head
    45–50°Mild
    50–55°Target
    >58°Over-tucked

    Aim for 50° first, then 53–55°. Above about 58° usually means you’re holding an exaggerated chin tuck, not a better posture. Changes under 3° between photos are within tapping error, so watch the trend over several check-ins.
